## Ingredients
- 1 lb smoked kielbasa (horseshoe ring), cut into bite-size pieces
- 8 cups unseasoned stuffing cubes
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, plus a little for the dish
- 1 1/2 cups diced yellow onion (1 large or 2 small)
- 1 cup diced celery (about 3 large stalks)
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 3/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9x13 baking dish. Put the stuffing cubes in a large bowl.
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the onion and celery until soft. Add the garlic for the last 2 minutes, then scrape everything into the bowl with the bread.
3. In the same skillet, brown the kielbasa over medium heat, 8 to 10 minutes. Add it to the bowl along with the fat in the pan.
4. Stir in the broth, egg, herbs, salt and pepper until the bread is evenly moistened.
5. Spread in the baking dish and bake uncovered 65 to 75 minutes, until the top is deep golden and crisp.

## Notes
- Adapted from Dietz & Watson's Horseshoe Kielbasa Stuffing.
- The source doesn't give a serving count; a 9x13 pan likely feeds 6 to 8 as a side, fewer as a main.
- Any smoked kielbasa works; the source uses its own brand.
