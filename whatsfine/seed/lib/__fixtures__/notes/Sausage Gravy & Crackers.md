## Immediate Thoughts

## Ingredients
- 1 lb bulk breakfast sausage
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 cups whole milk
- Lots of black pepper
- Salt to taste
- Saltine crackers, to serve

## Instructions
1. Brown and crumble the sausage in a skillet — keep all the fat.
2. Sprinkle the flour over, stir, and cook 1–2 minutes until it smells nutty.
3. Pour in the milk slowly, whisking, and simmer until it coats a spoon (5–8 min).
4. Pepper hard, salt to taste, and spoon over a plate of saltines.

## Notes
- By Jess Thackston, WhatsFine.
- Don't drain the fat. Lean sausage? Add a pat of butter before the flour; the roux needs the fat.
- Some people swear by a pinch of sage or a hit of hot sauce.
