## Immediate Thoughts

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups whole milk
- 4 oz dried chipped beef, torn
- Black pepper
- Toast, to serve

## Instructions
1. If the beef is very salty, rinse and pat dry. Tear into bite-size pieces.
2. Melt the butter, whisk in the flour, and cook 1 minute to a pale roux.
3. Add the milk slowly, whisking, until it thickens to a pourable gravy.
4. Stir in the beef, pepper generously, and spoon over hot toast.

## Notes
- By Jess Thackston, WhatsFine.
- Taste before salting; dried beef is cured and usually brings enough on its own.
- Build the gravy gently so it stays smooth, not gluey.
- Also good over biscuits or a split baked potato.
