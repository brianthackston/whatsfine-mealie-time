## Immediate Thoughts

## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 bag (10 oz) mini marshmallows
- 6 cups fruity crisped-rice cereal
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions
1. Before you start: Butter a 9×13-in pan
2. Melt the butter in a big pot over low heat.
3. Add the marshmallows and stir until just melted — no longer.
4. Off the heat, fold in the cereal and a pinch of salt until coated.
5. Press lightly into a buttered 9×13 pan, cool, and cut into squares.

## Notes
- By Jess Thackston, WhatsFine.
- Pull the marshmallows off the heat as soon as they melt; overcooked marshmallow sets hard and chewy.
- Press gently, just enough to hold together. Butter your hands first.
